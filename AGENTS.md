# Retrovizor — Santiago photo map

Static GitHub Pages photo journal for Tomislav Rupić, hosted from the `main` branch root at `https://tomislavrupic.github.io/retrovizor/`.

## Architecture
- `index.html`, `style.css`, `app.js`: accessible, responsive map, photo selection, archive, and lightbox.
- `photos.json`: 65 library images with relative assets: 48 original selections plus 17 explicitly supplied original-photo additions (`N01`–`N17`) with GPS and `DateTimeOriginal` capture times from 8–10 November 2026. N17 is the distinct sun-above-horizon frame from IMG_8209. N08 and N09 have source GPS privately audited but their public GPS is withheld because they show private homes; their public label is `Private location`. The map has 63 pins. Dated images sort by capture time. Do not invent metadata.
- `scouting.json`: 35 small-crew filming locations for 8–22 November 2026, checked 12 November. This is the Croatian master B-roll list for the remaining days, not a timed call sheet. Six `groups`, six `routes` (2–5 placeIds, walking/driving), per-location `shots` and shared `generalShots` extend the existing guide. Shot IDs are stable and progress uses guarded device-local keys `retrovizor-broll:chile-2026:LOCATION:SHOT`, separate from daily-plan keys. Area and photo-status filters combine; every route destination resolves from a card mapQuery/address. Retain all original 13 IDs and photo references. New candidates without photographs belong in the guide, not invented photo pins.
- `shoot-plan.json`: provisional 10 November van/foot production plan. The Shoot plan tab reuses the Leaflet map with a separate numbered marker-cluster layer (clusters explicitly say “N stops”). Ten stops use photo GPS, published venue locations or checked geocoding with coordinateSource provenance; private/unlocated interview holds have no directions. Checklists are device-local, keyed by date/stop/shot in guarded localStorage.
- `shoot-plan-day3.json`: 11 November call-sheet schedule for Estadio Croata and a private Providencia interview, with no B-roll stops.
- `shoot-plan-day4.json`: 12 November call sheet, with morning B-roll and two interviews. Six cards, nine checklist tasks and two public pins (hotel and Plaza Patricio Aylwin). B-roll, lunch and Hrvoj's venue remain TBC. The plaza pin is the bounding-box centre of OSM way 13910379, not an entrance. The existing Shoot plan tab loads all three files, defaults to the latest date and offers a day selector. `#plan&day=YYYY-MM-DD` and `#plan=STOP&day=YYYY-MM-DD` link to a specific day. Never derive production day numbers from array positions. Keep each day's checklist keys separate.
- `assets/photos`, `assets/thumbs`: resized derivatives with EXIF removed. Never publish original private source manifests.
- `sounds.json`: three field recordings and their photo associations, consumed by `initSounds()` in `app.js`. One native player with `preload="none"` stays available across views. Selecting a recording pauses the old one; playback requires a play gesture. Browsing photos does not switch or restart sound. Associated photos offer an explicit play button. Do not introduce parallel players or exact audio pins.
- `assets/audio/santiago-sunset.mp3`: user-confirmed sunset ambience, trimmed to source seconds 10.000–40.047667 (first 10 and final 20 seconds removed). Mono 48 kHz / 192 kb/s MP3, duration 30.047667 seconds, no gain/fades or embedded metadata. Associated with sunset-viewpoint photos N01/N15/N16/N17.
- `assets/audio/santiago-fountain.mp3` and `santiago-street.mp3`: full takes associated provisionally with N06 and N04 respectively because their capture times fall within the recordings' inferred local BWF time intervals. The UI explicitly labels recording locations unconfirmed. Recorder clock sync and microphone positions were not independently verified. Do not turn these associations into GPS claims. Original WAVs, source filenames and private clock/provenance audit stay outside the public repo.
- `assets/vendor`: pinned Leaflet 1.9.4 and MarkerCluster 1.5.3, licenses included.
- `assets/pix7-tokens.css`: canonical PIX-7 tokens copied from ALi Imagine. Keep semantic colors and media-first hierarchy. This web journal specializes the desktop guide; it does not need production diagnostics.

## Constraints
- Use original photo GPS; never invent locations or imply a recorded walking route.
- Preserve the original curated selection exclusions: no subway, window/dinner portraits, self-portraits, red boarding A10 or crowded cabin A22. The user explicitly requested the restaurant in the map: Bocanáriz food/wine details and Lastarria restaurant fronts are included; private receipts stay excluded.
- Those exclusions apply to the original curated film/photo selection. The user explicitly supplied Metro photographs `N10` and `N11` for the library; include those additions without restoring excluded original IDs. Preserve all 17 additions as supplied, with stripped-metadata public copies and no crop in the full-size view.
- The photo exclusions do not exclude new Metro filming plans: the user explicitly requested Metro B-roll. Preserve the existing photo collection while allowing Metro exterior shot tasks in the shoot plan.
- Do not publish local paths, camera identifiers, private provenance, source hashes, or credentials.
- Preserve all photo aspect ratios in the selected stage/lightbox. Thumbnails are intentionally cropped.
- No autoplay or decorative animation. Respect reduced motion and keyboard navigation.
- Public map uses OSM tiles with visible attribution, normal browser caching and Referer; no bulk prefetch.
- User specifically requested GitHub Pages. Do not deploy a parallel Sites instance.
- Version local app.js/style.css URLs in index.html and the shoot-plan.json fetch URL when releasing changes, so returning visitors do not receive a new HTML shell with cached older scripts/styles. The verifier strips URL queries before checking local assets.
- Every located photograph and scouting card links to Google Maps Directions. Photo links use original GPS camera coordinates; named guide locations use their verified address or named destination. Unlocated photos show “Location not recorded” and no directions link, including in the lightbox. Undated photos show “Date not recorded”; the upload date is not a capture date.
- Light, sound and scene notes are scouting suggestions. Source visitor hours from official pages, date the research, and distinguish visitor access from arranged filming access. Never claim bookings or permissions that have not been obtained.
- Keep the conflicting PDF interview holds provisional until the producer confirms them. Do not publish PDFs, private apartment addresses, crew contacts or source extracts. Do not infer a private residential location for Jorge. Directions open only for known public destinations.
- Day 3 follows the supplied dated call sheet: 10:20 call, 10:45 setup, 11:00–13:00 Guillermo, 13:00–14:30 lunch, 14:30 transfer, 15:00 setup, 15:30–17:30 Davor, 17:30 wrap/return. The user specified interviews only; do not add B-roll to this day. `privateLocation: true` suppresses stop pins, address display and directions, and suppresses private route links. The source JSON itself must never contain private address or contact details; hiding them in the UI is insufficient. Do not substitute a public Metro pin for the private home location.
- Day 4 retains the supplied times: 08:00 call, 08:15–10:00 B-roll, 10:00 transfer, 10:15 setup, David 10:45–13:00, lunch 13:00–14:00, 14:00 transfer, 14:30 setup, Hrvoj 15:00–17:00 and 17:00 wrap. Flag the conflict with the source's maximum two-hour interview duration including setup; do not silently shorten blocks. Do not infer Hrvoj's venue from his Iquique consular role. Keep all routes with TBC endpoints disabled and crew contacts out of public files.
